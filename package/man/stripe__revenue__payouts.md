#### Description

The `stripe revenue payouts` command lists recent payouts from your Stripe account to your connected bank account or debit card. Payouts represent the transfer of funds from your Stripe balance to your external account.

#### Usage

```bash
aux4 stripe revenue payouts [--limit <number>]
```

--limit  Number of payouts to list (default: 10)

#### Example

```bash
aux4 stripe revenue payouts --limit 20
```
